Support generic charts with configurable zoom, pan, cursor and tooltip controls, independent signal lanes, stepped interpolation, and per-point stroke widths and colors. Reuse LineSeries and timeline controls for both digital scopes and weather forecasts. Per-point styles allow compositions such as a variable-width cloud-cover strip with an explicit daylight indicator.
